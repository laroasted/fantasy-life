Fantasy Life Hub

Fantasy Life Hub is a scoreboard and draft tool for a fantasy league built around real-world outcomes instead of athletic box scores. Each season, league members draft picks across fifteen categories — NFL, NBA, MLB, NHL, NCAA Football, NCAA Basketball, ATP Tennis, international golf, Formula 1, MLS, film actor, film actress, musician, country, and company stock — and the site tracks how every pick performs over the course of the season.

The Scoreboard ranks every league member by category and by overall standing, updated automatically from live sports results, box office and awards data, music chart performance, and market data. The Draft tool runs the league's live snake draft and keeps a full pick history and recap. Trends charts each member's standing across the season, and the Calendar tracks upcoming events that affect scoring.
